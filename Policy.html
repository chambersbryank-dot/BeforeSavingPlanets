HOW WE CHECK FACTS

Editorial policy

Every date and era name on this site is written so a seventh grader and a geologist can read the same sentence.

Years are spelled out

We write “541 million years ago” and “4.54 billion years ago.” We do not leave a bare Ma or MA on a page. Ma is a specialist abbreviation. It is not a word a family can read aloud.

Two ages are easy to mix up, and we keep them apart. NASA dates the birth of the solar system to about 4.6 billion years ago. The U.S. Geological Survey dates the Earth itself to 4.54 billion years, with an uncertainty under one percent. This site uses both sentences, not one rounded slogan.

Era names follow the geologic time scale

Eon, era, period, and epoch names follow the international geologic time scale used by geological surveys and taught by the National Park Service. Boundaries move when the International Commission on Stratigraphy votes. When we name a boundary, we say “about” and give the year in words.

The story those names carry is the one in the National Park Service pages on geologic time and on fossils through geologic time, the Natural History Museum’s accounts of the solar system and the origin of life, NASA’s solar-system facts, and the National Geographic Education lesson “Formation of Earth,” written for seventh grade. Graphics on this site are captions for that story, not decoration.

What a picture is

A photograph is labeled as a photograph, with the maker and the licence. An artist’s interpretation of molten Earth or the first oceans is labeled as an interpretation by the Founder and SI, not a photograph and not a measured map. Flat maps from 541 million years ago onward are Scotese PALEOMAP reconstructions via GPlates (EarthByte), not a cartoon globe.

Careers we describe

Pages about study and work name real degree paths in geology, paleontology, and archaeology: undergraduate courses, field camp or field school, and graduate degrees. They do not treat fossil science as a treasure hunt.

Corrections

If a year, name, or credit is wrong, write to us from the Contact page. Created by the Founder and SI.

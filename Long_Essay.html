About this course

UCL ARCL0047 is titled A Detailed Study of a Selected Topic. It is 30 credits and normally only for Institute of Archaeology undergraduates. The dissertation is about 10,000 words, from fieldwork, museum work, laboratory work, analysis, or library and archive work. It states aims, methods, and results, and sets your judgment beside published ideas. A supervisor, a third-year tutor, and a handbook pace the UCL version. The module page names Dr Katie Hemer as module leader. This page teaches the shape using public sources, including pages on this site. It is not that dissertation, it assigns no UCL supervisor, and UCL does not endorse it.

Modeled on UCL ARCL0047. ARCH 410

Aims, methods, results

UCL says the written account is different from an essay that only reacts. You state what you set out to learn, how you looked, and what you found. Then you set your judgment next to published work. A question you cannot answer with the sources you have is too big. Shrink it.

  1. What three headings does UCL expect that a reaction essay can skip?
  2. What do you do when the question is bigger than the sources?

Public pages, cited

Allowed material is already public: a course on this site, a USGS or NASA or UCL page, a museum page. Quote the claim and the link. Do not add a field season. Do not open ground to “get data.” Library and archive work is one of the methods UCL names, and it is the method this page can support.

  1. Which UCL method can this page actually support?
  2. What do you not add in order to “get data”?

10,000 words is UCL’s length

UCL asks for about 10,000 words under supervision. A classroom version for this site can be shorter if the aims, methods, results, and citations are still there. Say which length you used. Do not pretend a short school essay is the UCL dissertation.

  1. What length does UCL name?
  2. What must a shorter version still contain?

No supervisor is assigned here

UCL’s timetable uses a dissertation supervisor and the third-year tutor. Dr Katie Hemer is named as module leader on the module page. None of them supervise this free outline. Hand a school essay to a teacher you already know. Do not email a UCL address from this page as if you were enrolled.

  1. Who paces the UCL dissertation, and who does not pace this page?
  2. Where should a school essay go?

Standards

NGSS text is the official English performance expectation. MS-ESS1-4 says 4.6 billion years. This site uses the USGS age, 4.54 billion years. Keep both numbers visible. C3 text is the College, Career, and Civic Life indicator, in English.

  • 3-LS4-1 Analyze and interpret data from fossils to provide evidence of the organisms and the environments in which they lived long ago.
  • MS-ESS1-4 Construct a scientific explanation based on evidence from rock strata for how the geologic time scale is used to organize Earth's 4.6-billion-year-old history.
  • D2.His.1.9-12 Evaluate how historical events and developments were shaped by unique circumstances of time and place as well as broader historical contexts.

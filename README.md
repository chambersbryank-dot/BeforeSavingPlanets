# Before Saving Planets (scaffold)
Sister site of www.savingplanets.com. "Saving Planets finds solutions without the doom. Before Saving Planets is where Earth's story begins."
Scaffold for review only: all copy is placeholder; pages are `noindex` until launch.
Build: `python3 scripts/build.py` (strings for en/fr/es/ar/it/he live in the script). Shared CSS/JS copied from Saveworld.

Do not put cartoon Earth globes back, and do not put Dig Quest back in the menu or on the homepage. Flat PALEOMAP maps, lava and first-ocean stills, and NASA photographs stay. See `.cursor/rules/no-cartoon-earth.mdc`.
Deploy: Cloudflare Pages (direct upload via wrangler).

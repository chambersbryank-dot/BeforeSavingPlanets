Learn

Start with two careers: geologist and archaeologist. Rocks and time on one path. People and their things on the other.

Photograph behind the title: NASA, Apollo 17, the Blue Marble, 7 December 1972. Public domain. Not a cartoon globe.

  • Earth materials

    Minerals, rocks, and the processes that make them.

  • Deep human past

    Material evidence of people, set in deep time.

Three starts

  1. Read a rock — Say whether it cooled, settled, or changed while solid. Most fossils sit in the settled ones.
  2. A layer is a page — Younger usually lies on older, unless the stack was turned. A formation is rock. A period is time.
  3. A thing needs its layer — Archaeology is not treasure. Without the layer, most of the story is gone.

Kids

Short fossil facts, with the map one click away. The map stays off the top bar.

Photograph behind the title: NASA, Apollo 17, the Blue Marble, 7 December 1972. Public domain. Not a cartoon globe.

  • Trilobites — Trilobites lived in the seas and are gone. They are much older than dinosaurs.
  • Ferns in rock — A fossil plant means plants grew there. Most fossils sit in sedimentary rock, the kind that settled.
  • Dinosaurs — Dinosaurs lived on land. A giant swimmer from the same ages is not automatically a dinosaur.
  • Ice Age — Mammoths are far closer to us in time than a Tyrannosaurus is to a mammoth. Tyrannosaurus lived near 66 million years ago.

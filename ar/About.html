Before Saving Planets

The aim

Show the fossil record in plain language, with the source next to the claim. No invented body length. No cartoon Earth.

What we cover

Dinosaurs, mammals, animals of the air, animals of the sea, a world map of finds, and Deep Time. Education pages outline geology and archaeology.

The mission

Saving Planets shows solutions for the living world. This site shows the record those solutions sit on: climates, ecosystems, and life through deep time.

Write to us

hello@savingplanets.com. That is the Saving Planets inbox until a Before Saving Planets address exists.

How a page earns its place

A source you can open

A date or a claim on a finished page links to a paper, a museum, a survey, or a park service page.

A picture is labeled

A photograph says who made it. An artist’s reconstruction says Created by the Founder and SI. A map says which model drew the coastlines.

No invented measure

A body length appears only when a cited source gives that length. A file note from an artist is not a measurement.

Help goes to the museum

Donate and volunteer buttons open the museum that holds the fossil. This site is free and asks for nothing for itself.

Who builds this

The Founder

Sets the direction and approves the pages.

SI

Researches, drafts, and checks the sources. The credit line is Created by the Founder and SI.

Volunteers

Language corrections are welcome at hello@savingplanets.com.

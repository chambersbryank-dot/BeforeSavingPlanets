الحياة عبر الزمن

ثدييات

الثدييات الموجودة أصلًا في هذا الموقع. لـ Smilodon صفحة كاملة. البطاقات الأخرى تفتح قطعة الأحفورة أو المصدر المستخدم أصلًا في خريطة الزمن العميق.

خلفية فقط: الرخام الأزرق، الأرض من أبولو 17 في 7 ديسمبر 1972. ناسا، ملكية عامة. ليست أحد الحيوانات.

جمل كل حيوان ما زالت بالإنجليزية، بانتظار المراجعة.

الزمن العميق. فعّلوا هناك شريحة Mammals لتروا هذه المجموعة تتحرك.

Smilodon

A saber-toothed cat, not a tiger. The Florida Museum says more than 2,000 skulls of Smilodon fatalis come from La Brea, and it dates the species to about 700,000 to 11,000 years ago. The La Brea museum says its collections represent the last 50,000 years of southern California life. That span is the local collection, not the whole life of the species.

Waco mammoths

A nursery herd of Columbian mammoths in Texas. The National Park Service dates that herd’s death to between 65,000 and 72,000 years ago. The timeline mark is 68,000 years, inside that range, not a single measured year.

Nimbadon

A wombat-like marsupial from Riversleigh. UNESCO dates those mammals to about 30 to 10 million years ago. Black and others, 2012, place Nimbadon lavarackorum in a middle Miocene cave and read the skeleton as a climber. The picture of a mother and young in a tree is that paper’s restoration, not a photograph. The timeline mark is 15 million years, inside the UNESCO span, not a single measured year.

Lucy

Australopithecus afarensis from Ethiopia, about 3.2 million years ago.

La Brea Tar Pits

Asphalt trapped Ice Age animals in Los Angeles. The La Brea museum says its collections represent the last 50,000 years of southern California life. This note does not turn that span into an ending at 10,000 years.

Wadi Al-Hitan

Whales that still had hind legs, from the Late Eocene of Egypt, about 40 to 37 million years ago. The picture is a fossil jaw. This card has no living restoration.

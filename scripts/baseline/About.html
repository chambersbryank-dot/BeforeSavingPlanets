Saving Planets, Minus the Doom

The Aim

Show a fix, show the number, and invite the next idea. No guilt. No apocalypse slide.

What We Cover

Ocean cleanup, reforestation at three trees for every one removed, and solar-powered computing in orbit that uses no water for cooling.

The Mission

We aim to be a centralized hub for educating people around the world about current solutions that are making a difference each and every day.

Our Solutions Are Based on the Following Criteria

Hands-On Work

The organization must do the work itself — cleanup crews, planting teams, barrier installations, or operating hardware. We feature doers, not just funders or promoters.

Published, Verifiable Numbers

Impact must be backed by specific, checkable figures — kilograms removed, trees planted, devices deployed, debris captured — with a source we can cite.

A Clear Donate Path

Visitors should be able to support the work directly. A donation page, a product-funded model, or a transparent get-involved route all count.

Solutions, Not Advocacy

The focus is on organizations that remove, restore, or build. Lobbying groups, awareness campaigns without measurable action, and pure research projects are out of scope.

How We Feature Solutions from Around the World

Saving Planets showcases solutions from any country that genuinely help both people and nature.

We judge each effort on its own record, not on the government of the country it works in.

We feature efforts that are independent and transparent about how they use their funds.

Featuring a project doesn't mean we're partners with it or endorse it; we share it so people can learn and find ways to help.

We link Donate buttons only to organizations that publish audited finances or carry a recognized charity rating, so your gift goes to the cause and not to corruption.

Every fact we share comes from a source we cite.
